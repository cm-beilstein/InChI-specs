InChI stereo extensions

Four extensions of InChI for stereochemistry, all behind the option -EnhancedStereochemistry (prefix InChI=1B/). Per extension: the technical specification, an explanation for chemists, old-vs-new examples, and how the new InChI is built step by step. Comments are welcome on every molecule and explanation section (GitHub account needed).
